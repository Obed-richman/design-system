Brand marks

The Zego logo and the product lockups — Zego Design System

Logo

The full wordmark and the compact mark (the Z, for tight spaces and app icons — e.g. the side-nav header). Both are one tone, painted with currentColor, so they take the colour of whatever they sit in — navy on a light surface, white on the brand header. That is why the header on this page can reuse the same file.

Product lockups

Zego wordmark over the product name. Two tones — the wordmark in Zego navy #00166C and the product name in #A458FF — so unlike the logo these carry their own colour and cannot be recoloured by their container. Place them on a light surface; there is no dark-background version in 003 yet.

Sub-brand lockups — single tone

The ZEGO wordmark stacked over the product name in one tone, painted with currentColor. Unlike the two-tone product lockups above, these recolour to fit their surface — navy on a light background, white on a dark one. Reach for these where a monochrome mark is needed (dense UI, single-colour print, on-brand headers).
