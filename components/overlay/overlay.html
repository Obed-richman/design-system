Overlay

A wash over the whole screen. It does one job: take the page out of play so whatever sits on top of it is plainly the thing to deal with. Modal puts a dialogue over one.

The three washes, over the same page. Dark for a light page, Light for a dark one, Darker when what sits on top has to win outright.

Light — overlay/light-low

Dark — overlay/dark-low

Darker — overlay/dark-medium
